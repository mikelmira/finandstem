# Fin & Stem Content Roadmap

An outside-in review of the site as a working aquascaper would judge it, and a prioritised plan for the gaps that stop it being the hobby's primary reference. Written September 2026.

## Where the site is already strong

- **Deep catalogue:** 92 fish, 83 plants, plus shrimp, mosses, snails, substrates and hardscape, all cross-referenced.
- **Best-in-class tools:** the tank planner with a stocking gauge and water-column view, the compatibility engine, side-by-side compare, and the calculators.
- **The algae ID hub** is genuinely differentiated.

The site wins on the *what* (which species or material, and does it fit my tank). It is thin on the *how* and the *when it goes wrong*, which are the pages people return to.

## The gaps, in priority order

1. **How to cycle a tank.** No dedicated hub, only scattered mentions. Fishless, fish-in, and plant-heavy silent cycling; what the numbers do week by week; how to read a test kit. The most-searched entry point in the hobby.
2. **Fish and shrimp disease and health.** No hub. Mirror the algae ID pattern: ich, fin rot, dropsy, velvet, columnaris, shrimp molt failure, plus quarantine and a plant/shrimp-safe medication guide. High volume, high repeat use.
3. **Plant nutrient deficiency diagnosis.** The missing twin of the algae hub. Visual iron/N/P/K/Ca/Mg/CO2 diagnosis. Nobody in the hobby does this well. **(In progress.)**
4. **Water chemistry reference.** pH, KH, GH, TDS, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, how they interrelate, target ranges by tank type, and how to move them safely.
5. **Aquascaping design and composition.** Practical how-to, not just history: rule of thirds, focal point, depth, negative space, and step-by-step Iwagumi / Nature / Dutch / Walstad / biotope.
6. **CO2 setup depth.** Beyond the drop-checker calculator: regulator, diffuser, bubble count, injection, safety.
7. **Maintenance routines and a symptom-based troubleshooting hub** ("cloudy water", "gasping fish", "melting plants", "snail outbreak") that routes into the right diagnostic page.
8. **Build journals / case studies.** The project brief's own defensible moat, and there is no builds section. Real tanks over time, with original photos, are what competitors cannot copy.

## What to expand rather than add

- **Thin catalogues:** shrimp (15), mosses (13), snails (12) are small next to fish and plants. More neocaridina/caridina morphs, sulawesi, filter feeders, and the popular carpets, stems and epiphytes.
- **Equipment** is only four overview topics. Needs depth: heater sizing, flow/turnover, surface skimmers, UV, air, auto top-off, timers, with real PAR and GPH figures.
- **Per-entry depth:** consistent propagation and breeding sections, disease susceptibility per fish, deficiency signs per plant.
- **A glossary** of hobby terms.

## Suggested build order

1. Plant-deficiency diagnostic hub (reuses the algae-ID pattern, fills a real gap). **← building now**
2. Fish/shrimp disease diagnostic hub.
3. Cycling / start-here hub.
4. Water chemistry reference.
5. Aquascaping design and composition hub.
6. Build journals (depends on original photos).

The two diagnostic hubs plus cycling capture the highest-intent "help me, something is wrong" traffic and reuse patterns already proven on the site.
